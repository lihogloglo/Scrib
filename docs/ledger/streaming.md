## Standing facts

- Streaming spikes are render-thread spikes, not scheduler cost: stages are time-budgeted and cheap (45 us per tick); the costs are downstream (origin-shift re-collection, mesh-add bursts, `VisibilityRange` walks, bind-group rebuilds).
- An origin shift is a one-off full re-collection in Bevy (every `GlobalTransform` changes), 3-4 ms render + 2-3 ms big_space; cell size stays 256 m; nothing else structural may share its frames (guard: 0.05 s before, 2 frames after, max 6).
- The stutter gate (<= 1/min) has never passed on the borderless uncapped setup; the walk reads ~14-18/min, the flight 21-27/min (2026-10-12); the lazy floating origin and byte-paced mesh uploads were the next levers and were never built.
- Bevy's upload limiter must stay off (stale offsets on deferred upload); pace loads by meshes in flight (`max_loading_meshes`) instead.
- Pacing results depend on the setup: GPU-bound 1440p on the 1080p panel hides render-thread spikes; borderless at the display's resolution, uncapped, is the honest setup; Fifo 60 Hz hides everything.
- Some stalls (30-1600 ms, all counters inflated) and `present_frames` 10-15 ms appear in before and after runs alike: machine/driver, not streaming.
- A cell's static scene batch slots are written at stream-in only; the scene needs one full slot rewrite per origin shift.
