## Standing facts

- The IK stack is the industry's (UE Foot Placement, Stride and Orientation Warping, Inertialization); the base fed to it is the problem, not the ops.
- 2026-11-12 audit main finding: Morrowind walk/run clips play far off their authored speeds (walk ~0.85x, run 1.2-1.4x, sprint 2.2x), with no start/stop/pivot. Owner decided: generated walk/jog/sprint cycles plus starts, stops, pivots and turns in place for the modern preset; Morrowind clips stay for Classic.
- Shipped practice keeps play rate near 0.8-1.25 and swaps clips outside it; decided rate clamp 0.8-1.25 (Lyra).
- Player foot IK was never fed until P-IK1 (2026-11-11); every confirmed foot check (AL-9, AN-1, AN-21) was on NPC walkers. Nothing about the player's walk is signed off (AL-2, AL-3 awaiting, PL-7 partial, PL-8 awaiting).
- The measurement tests compare IK against a twin without IK, so they cannot see clip-speed, gait-switch or turn-blend causes.
- Do not retune slope ops (P-IK3, P-IK4, lean, pelvis speed cap) before the new gait set exists: tuning them on clips about to be replaced is the trap.
- P-IK3 (pelvis bob) and P-IK4 (head stays level in `Lean`) are specified but not built; the locomotion-rethink slices L0-L8 have no ROADMAP line.
- Animation changes need `bevyrrowind bake` before the owner tests; headless tests bake their own cache.
