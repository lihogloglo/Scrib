CB-6: spammable roll awaiting owner

The cause in code. The roll had no input buffer, so every press during a roll was dropped. The tap/hold button only fired on release.

  • A press during the second half of a roll (from 0.25 s) is now buffered and fires at the cancel point, so mashing chains rolls.
  • While a move accepts a dodge (a roll, or an attack's recovery), the button rolls on press.
  • From standing, tap still rolls on release and hold still sprints, as you decided on 2026-10-27. Holding through a roll keeps sprinting.
  • The roll plays faster (rate 1.25 to 1.6) over about the same distance, and the pop when a roll restarts itself is fixed.

PL-10: slope lean awaiting owner

The cause in code. The lean pitched the trunk by up to 26°. The head took the full angle, and the spine rotation carried the arms with it, so the hands ended up behind the back. Jog and sprint got the full lean too.

  • The lean is now small: 0.3 rad per unit of grade, capped at about 9°.
  • It fades with speed, full up to 2.5 m/s and gone at 6.5 m/s.
  • The head and upper arms are turned back toward their pose before the lean, which the industry calls head stabilisation and arm gravity compensation.
  • NPC walkers share the same graph, so they get the same fix.

What the research found

The notes are in docs/research/gameplay-2026-11-25/. Claims a note marks "from memory" were not checked against a source.

Animation (anim.md, anim-diagnosis.md)

  • 100STYLE is the best free locomotion source we don't use yet. It is real motion capture under CC BY 4.0, so it can ship in the repo, and it has walks and runs in every direction, idles and transitions. Recommendation: cook it into the locomotion slots and keep Kimodo for ambient filler.
  • KayKit Character Animations (CC0, 161 clips including melee, block and dodge) is new to our docs and nobody has judged it yet.
  • Morrowind's own attack clips (chop, slash and thrust per weapon type, cast, block) are already loaded in the graph as upper-body clips, but the player doesn't use them. Their text keys give hit and cancel timing for free.
  • OrientationWarp and LookAt exist but aren't wired up, and an acceleration lean is missing. Starts and stops can use inertialized transitions with no new clips. Motion matching should wait.
  • Licences: Bandai Namco is non-commercial. LaFAN1, Mixamo and CMU are bring-your-own only.

Combat (combat.md)

  • The plumbing already matches the Souls-like standard: fixed tick, timing windows, swept weapon hits, hit-stop.
  • There's no enemy decision layer. The combat AI slice (AI15) is one line in the plan. The standard is attack tokens as in Doom 2016, plus spacing bands, reactions scaled by skill, and morale.
  • Reverse two non-goals: add parry with riposte, and replace "every hit staggers" with a poise system that works the same for player and enemies. Damage should become typed, with status buildup.
  • Ranged and magic need a delivery layer in the framework, with effects ported in the adapter.

NPC AI (ai.md)

  • Keep the stack: directives, utility drives and HTN match what Guerrilla ships. Two claims in our industry table are wrong: Kingdom Come uses behaviour trees, not GOAP, and the Dying Light 2 and Transformers HTN claims have no source.
  • Reorder the plan. Move smart objects (AI14) ahead of combat AI (AI15) and ship a thin "day loop" first. Perception (AI8) should have graded awareness, search of the last known position, and shared alerts.
  • Missing from the plan: barks and chatter, followers, NPC reactions to weather, time of day and the player, and bounded gossip. Drop needs as a subsystem; two or three drive considerations cover them.
  • No AI crate fits as the core.

Navigation (nav.md)

  • The ground stack (Recast tiles and a Detour corridor) is the standard; keep it. The vendored navmesh-rs is 0.2.2, upstream is 0.2.6, and it already has tile cache and crowd modules.
  • Fliers and swimmers are the biggest gap. Cliff racers need a per-tile clearance field, and fish need a water-volume layer.
  • The generated 10x world needs runtime tile generation with a disk cache. Long-range routing should run on a road and door graph with travel time for NPCs travelling off-screen. NPC jumps and climbs should reuse the player's mantle and vault.

Dialogue and quests (dialogue-quests.md)

  • The biggest quest blocker: per the wiki, object, global and start MWScripts don't run yet. That matters more than any missing function family.
  • Don't adopt Yarn or Ink as the format. Use our own small scene model with stable line IDs, plus a Yarn importer (bevy_yarnspinner 0.10 supports Bevy 0.20).
  • Add a Valve-style rule database (fuzzy pattern matching) for dialogue, barks and ambient lines. Morrowind's dialogue filters compile into it.
  • Presentation modes: classic topic window, cinematic with an automatic camera, and barks. Text-only lines get a procedural talking animation. An LLM is a seam only.

Questions for you

These are only questions that change what the player can do, filtered from the notes. Each has a recommended answer, and "yes to all recommended" is a valid reply.

  1. Roll button on keyboard: a dedicated roll key, with sprint on a separate hold, so a roll from standing is instant too? Gamepad keeps Elden Ring's tap to roll, hold to sprint. Recommended: yes. Today rolls are instant during moves, but from standing they still wait for the key's release.
  2. Poise: light hits flinch without interrupting a swing, and only heavy hits or a broken poise bar stagger, for player and enemies alike? Recommended: yes, as in Elden Ring. Every-hit-interrupts stays the Classic rule.
  3. Parry: a well-timed guard becomes a parry, with a riposte on the staggered target? Recommended: yes, a tight window on the same button.
  4. Backstabs and ripostes: big damage with a short finisher animation, no cutscene? Recommended: yes.
  5. Moving while casting or drawing a bow: Recommended: a slowed walk for bows and small spells, rooted for big spells.
  6. Friendly fire: Recommended: area spells and projectiles hurt anyone, melee doesn't hit allies, co-op friendly fire off by default.
  7. Clip shipping: ship CC0 clips (UAL, KayKit) and CC BY clips (100STYLE) with an attribution file, everything else bring-your-own? Recommended: yes.
  8. NPC walk styles by age, mood or role, from 100STYLE? Recommended: yes, later.
  9. Companions: a command wheel (follow, wait, hold fire, engage), and companions knocked down rather than killed by default? Recommended: yes to both.
  10. Gossip: reputation and crime news spread from NPC to NPC instead of an instant faction-wide bounty? Recommended: gossip in Modern, instant in Classic.
  11. Suspicion meter over NPCs? Recommended: a Modern option, off in Classic.
  12. Crowd extras (generic pedestrians) in big cities? Recommended: later, off in Classic.
  13. Free-text or voice talk with NPCs through a plugin? Recommended: a seam only, off by default, never changes quests or rewards.
  14. Fliers and swimmers path through the real 3D world instead of fixed flight paths? Recommended: yes.
  15. Travelling NPCs appear on the roads near you and advance by travel time off-screen? Recommended: yes.
  16. Runtime nav changes: a collapsed wall or a built house makes NPCs re-path? Recommended: yes, for authored or destructible geometry only.
  17. On-demand nav tiles for generated worlds, cached to disk, as a fallback only? This is an exception to "nothing bakes during play". Recommended: yes.
  18. Dialogue default: cinematic (camera, choice wheel) for new games, while Morrowind keeps the topic window by default with Cinematic as a setting? Recommended: yes.
  19. Quest markers: none by default for Morrowind (directions in the text), a compass for the generic framework, and a setting to switch? Recommended: yes.
  20. Interrupted conversations: combat or crime cancels a conversation, which resumes with a bark? Recommended: yes.
  21. Voice: ship text-only lines with a procedural talking animation, and no TTS in the framework? Recommended: yes.
