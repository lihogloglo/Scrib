## Verdict

The delta is small and narrow: **50 commits, 78 files, +25k lines, all of it Skyrim actor work**. There is
**no terrain change at all** since `805777d` (the terrain-merge and Metal work is already in the previous
audit), and nothing on lighting, shadows, GI, water, sky, foliage, UI or scripting. They are still on Bevy
0.19. What landed is their first actor pipeline: legacy `NiSkinInstance` skin export, body/hands/feet
composition on a shared skeleton, an actor model catalog, Havok idle/walk/run clips decoded to GLB, FaceGen
head rehosting onto the body skeleton, a Skyrim skin material (model-space normals, FaceGen tint) and a gait
selector with engine-driven translation. We are well ahead on every runtime part of this (skinning, smooth
bodies, assembly with merge/LRU, morphs, tints, anim graph, locomotion, ragdoll: `sf_character*`, `sf_anim*`,
`sf_controller`, `mw_actors`, `mw_nif/src/skin.rs`). Their actor runtime is "one gait clip chosen by speed,
translation by `speed*dt`, no AI/nav/input", roughly our first spike. Almost everything is Skyrim file format
(Havok 2010.2 hkx, BSDismember, MSN maps) and so irrelevant to Morrowind data. **Nothing is worth porting as
code now.** Two cheap technique takeaways (a skin-export rule checklist for our census tests, and an
actor-readiness check in streaming). On terrain: nothing to borrow.
