# Foam look: texture, scale, layers, lighting (2026-11-17)

Research only, no code run. Scope: how foam LOOKS. Where it is born is in `surf-and-foam-2026-11-17.md`
(lines 96-152). Verified-by-fetch items are marked [F]; items from memory of talks and source are marked [M]
and need a check before they go in a spec.

## Summary

- Our foam is one procedural cellular "lace" with 1.4 m cells (`lace_cell_m`, `sf_water/src/shore.rs:191`),
  shaded as `mix(body, albedo, foam)` with roughness 0.9 (`water_material.wesl:350-351`). No foam texture exists
  (no `foam` asset under `bevyrrowind/assets`, which only has `animation/`; the only `foam` hits in
  `sf_water_bevy/src` are tunable plumbing).
- "Painted" has three causes, all in the shading and the scale, not the mask:
  1. **One scale, and it is the wrong one.** Real foam has bubble texture at 0.5-10 cm, clumps and holes at
     10-50 cm, streaks and rafts at 0.5-3 m. A single 1.4 m cell pattern is the streak scale only: from the
     player's height each "cell" is a large flat blob with no inner detail. Every good project layers at least
     two scales.
  2. **Flat albedo.** Painted = constant colour inside a hard-ish mask. Real foam is a height field of bubbles:
     crevices are darker (self-occlusion), tops bright, thin foam shows the water through the holes.
  3. **No lighting detail.** Foam must carry its own normal (from its height) and a soft, wrap-diffuse look.
     Crest does this ("Foam 3D Lighting") [F].
- Recommendation: a baked tileable **foam atlas texture** (we generate it ourselves, CC0 by construction),
  sampled at three world scales with two-phase flow advection, thresholded by our existing masks with a feather,
  lit from a packed height+normal. About 5 taps per foam fragment. Two slices, each with an A/B switch.

Plain reading of the table: nobody ships a single-scale procedural cell pattern as the final look. The
consistent recipe is **sim or analytic mask, then threshold against a tiled foam texture with a feather, then
light it from its own height, plus a subsurface bubble layer**.

## Texture source and licence

- **Crest's foam texture.** The Crest repository is MIT [F]. The asset lives in the package folder; confirm the
  exact file and that it carries no separate licence before copying [M, unverified path]. Even so, the style is
  tuned to Crest's blending; we do not need it.
- **ambientCG / Poly Haven (CC0).** ambientCG has a "Foam" and "Foam Substance" category, but those are
  sponge/styrofoam and acoustic foam (not sea foam); no sea-foam material found [F]. Poly Haven: none found.
  So a CC0 photo sea-foam scan is not available from the two usual sources.
- **AI/stock sites** (aitextured.com "FCL", SketchUp Texture Club): custom or login licences. Skip.
- **Recommendation: generate our own.** A small offline tool (Rust, in `tools/` or a `sf_water` example, run
  once; one-time bake of seconds, fits the "bake only as a one-time step" rule) writes a tileable
  1024x1024 RGBA8 (or BC7 after) texture. Our code, GPL-compatible, no provenance problem. A procedural foam can
  match a photo only if it is **bubble-packed, not noise-thresholded**: that is the key difference from our
  lace.

### Bake recipe (the texture)

Foam photographs are packed spheres of many sizes with thin films between them, plus empty patches.
1. Scatter points on a torus with Poisson-ish spacing at 3 radius classes (relative 1 : 2.5 : 6, e.g. 1.5 mm,
   4 mm, 10 mm at the fine tile scale), several thousand bubbles; each bubble a dome height `sqrt(1-(d/r)^2)`,
   combine with max (pile) so smaller bubbles sit in gaps of larger ones.
2. Film lines: `F2 - F1` of the same points gives the thin ridges between bubbles; mix a little in.
3. Clumping: multiply bubble density by a low-frequency tileable noise (period = 1/4 of the tile) so there
   are holes and rafts, not an even carpet. Keep that noise in channel A.
4. Pack: **R,G = normal xy** (from the height gradient, strength baked), **B = height** (0 crevice, 1 dome
   top), **A = clump density**. One tap gives mask, lighting and clumping. Height is linear (not sRGB).
5. Mips: generate with a box filter, but renormalise the normal each level and keep the mean of B; far away
   the sampler blends to a flat coverage, which we handle in the shader (below).
6. Size: 1024x1024 RGBA8 = 4 MB, 5.3 MB mipped; BC7 1.3 MB. Anisotropic 8x for grazing views.

## Scale: why 1.4 m is wrong, and what to use

Measured features of real foam [M, from photographs and Crest/UE scales, not exact]: bubbles 1-10 mm, visible
foam "cells" 1-5 cm, clumps and holes 10-50 cm, streaks along the flow 0.5-3 m long and 5-30 cm wide, bore
band 1-5 m wide. Our 1.4 m "holes" are the streak/raft scale; they read as large flat spots.

Screen footprint at our target (1440p DLSS Quality renders at 960p; vertical FOV about 60 degrees): one pixel
is about 2.4 cm at 20 m, 6 cm at 50 m, 1.2 cm at 10 m (looking square-on; grazing is worse by 1/sin). So
features under about 5 cm alias beyond about 20 m. Detail has to be tiered by distance:

| Layer | World tile | Feature size | Visible out to | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fine | 2.5 m (2.4 mm/texel) | 0.5-3 cm bubbles | about 15 m (fade by mip) | close up bubble texture and normal |
| Medium | 7.3 m, rotated 31 deg | 2-10 cm | about 60 m | the main read |
| Coarse | 23 m (not a multiple of the others) | clumps and holes, 0.3-1.5 m | everywhere | modulates density, breaks tiling |

Tile sizes are coprime-ish so the three never realign. Keep `lace_cell_m` semantics as the **coarse
modulation** scale, not the visible pattern (rename later).

## Anti-tiling and motion

- Two scales plus rotation already hide tiling (the Godot two-sample trick [F search] is the cheapest form).
- Advection: foam must travel with the water, not stand still or slide at one speed. Use the standard
  **two-phase flow-map scroll** (Valve Portal 2 [M]; the gamedev.net demo [F search]): each layer samples at
  `uv + flow * phase` and `uv + flow * (phase + 0.5)`, blended by `|1-2*frac(t)|`. Flow here is the swash/bore
  shoreward direction times a speed, with a small domain warp from the coarse layer. That is 2 taps per
  layer; to hold the budget, run the two-phase only on the medium layer, the fine and coarse layers scroll
  at a fixed drift.
- Stretch along flow in the surf zone (scale u by 0.4-0.6) so bore foam is streaky; isotropic at contact lines
  and on calm water.

## How the masks drive the texture

Today the mask (`foam` 0-1 from `surf_foam` age profile, shore band, contact) goes straight into
`mix(body, foam_rgb, foam)`. Instead, treat the mask as **coverage** and let the texture decide where inside it
the foam is:

```
density = texture_composite(uv layers)         // 0-1 from B height x A clump
edge    = 1 - coverage                          // coverage = existing foam mask
foam    = smoothstep(edge - feather, edge + feather, density)   // Crest "Foam Feather" [M: the pattern]
```

- Fresh foam (coverage near 1) fills almost everything; aged foam (coverage falling) keeps only the dome tops
  and clump centres, leaving holes: this is automatically bubble-shaped, not blobby. This replaces the
  `lace_fresh`/`lace_aged` width pair.
- Feather is the existing `feather` tunable (re-unit it to density units, default about 0.08).
- Thickness for lighting and transparency = `saturate((density - edge) / feather)` (0 at the foam edge, 1 deep
  inside).
- Contact foam: same formula with `coverage = 1 - vertical/contact`; a thin line at the edge falls out of the
  threshold.

## Lighting recipe (kills "painted")

1. **Normal** from the packed RG (a bumped normal in the water's tangent frame), strength = thickness x
   `relief` tunable (replaces the lace gradient at `water_material.wesl:343`). Blend with the water normal by
   thickness.
2. **Albedo by height and thickness**: `albedo = mix(0.55, 0.9, height) * mix(1.0, 0.7, ao)` where ao is
   (1 - height) at the crevices; 0.9 on dome tops is Whitlock's number we already cite.
3. **Translucency**: thin foam (thickness < 0.4) mixes with the water body colour by `1 - thickness` so holes
   and edges show the water (we already have `aged_opacity`; drive it by thickness instead of only age).
4. **Wrap diffuse + sun sub-surface**: use `(n.l + w)/(1+w)` with w=0.5 for the diffuse term and add a small
   forward-scatter lobe toward the sun (cheap `pow(max(dot(v,-l),0), 4) * thickness`). Foam in sun should have
   a warm bright core, not a clipping white.
5. **Roughness** 0.9 stays; no specular spike except from the Fresnel of the water below.
6. **Bubbles layer (Crest "Foam Bubbles" [F])**: the existing `glow` term, additionally sampled with the
   medium texture offset along the view direction by 0.05 m x thickness (parallax) so the under-roller turquoise
   shows depth. Optional in slice 2.

## Distance filtering

Per layer: `fade = 1 - smoothstep(0.5, 1.5, texel_footprint_in_features)` using the derivative of world uv.
When a layer fades out, replace its contribution by that layer's **mean coverage** (a constant, baked) so
foam does not vanish or switch pattern; do not rely on mip averaging of a thresholded value (it drifts toward
0.5, washes to grey-white mush). Far field (> about 150 m) = mask x mean coverage only (zero taps).
Alpha-to-coverage is not needed; water is not MSAA-bound at DLSS.

## Recommended slices

**FL-1 foam atlas + layered sampling (one deliverable)**
- Offline generator (tool or example, run once, output committed or built on first run: 1024^2, 5 MB) plus
  sampling in `water_material.wesl` replacing the lace term for the **look** only (the masks are unchanged).
- Three scales as in the table, two-phase on the medium layer, threshold-with-feather, distance fades to
  mean coverage.
- Tunables (declared in `SurfFoam`, with unit, range, source): `foam_tile_fine_m` 2.5, `foam_tile_med_m` 7.3,
  `foam_tile_coarse_m` 23, `foam_flow_speed_mps`, `foam_stretch`, `foam_fade_start/end` (pixels per feature).
- Switch: `--off foamtex` returns to the current lace (keep it; A/B arm).
- Cost: about 5 taps (fine 1, medium 2, coarse 1, plus 1 bubble optional) x water-foam fragments only (the
  branch is already under `foam > 0`). Foam covers maybe 5-15% of a surf scene; at 960p internal that is
  about 0.05-0.15 Mpix taps x 5, under 0.1 ms. The 3x3 cellular lace it replaces is probably costlier (9
  hash evaluations per cell lookup). Expect parity to slightly faster. Report ms per preset in the ledger
  (`docs/ledger/water.md`) from an interleaved `perf suite --arm`.
- Visual check entry: surf scene on a beach and a rock/pier contact, camera at 2 m, 10 m and 40 m height,
  look for bubble detail up close, no repeat at 50 m, no shimmer on camera move.

**FL-2 lit foam (one deliverable)**
- Packed normal bump, height albedo/AO, thickness translucency, wrap and forward scatter, under-roller
  bubble parallax tap. Replaces the lace gradient normal and constant 0.8 albedo.
- Tunables: `foam_normal_strength`, `foam_ao`, `foam_wrap`, `foam_scatter`, `foam_bubble_parallax_m`.
- Switch: `--off foamlit` = flat albedo and the old gradient normal.
- Cost: no new taps for normal/albedo (they ride in the same sample); +1 tap for bubble parallax;
  extra ALU of about 40 ops per foam fragment. Expect under 0.1 ms.

Order: FL-1 first (scale and pattern are the big wrongness); FL-2 second. Both are look-only, so the owner
signs off in his batch (no automated image checks).
