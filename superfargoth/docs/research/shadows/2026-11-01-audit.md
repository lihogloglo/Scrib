# Shadow audit 2026-11-01: lamp shadows on characters, reach, camera-moving bands

Read-only audit (three sub-agents, orchestrator verified every CONFIRMED claim in source). Owner complaints
(2026-10-30 pass): (1) torches and lanterns don't shadow NPCs or the player, mostly in cities and in the Census
office; (2) some shadows appear only a few metres away (SH-13); (3) in some builds dark bands appear everywhere and
move with the camera (RO-11), plus a bright rim around characters (RO-12). Bevy 0.20.0 / 0.20.1; no 0.21 yet.

## 1. Lamp and torch shadows on characters: a `RenderLayers` mismatch (CONFIRMED)

Bevy builds a point light's caster list from meshes whose `RenderLayers` intersect the **light's** layers
(`bevy_light-0.20.0/src/lib.rs:609-613`; a mesh without the component is layer 0). Who sits where:

| entity | layers | set in |
|---|---|---|
| placed lamp / lantern / sconce light | `layer(N)` (the worldspace's layer) | `mw_exterior_bevy/src/exterior.rs:509` |
| world meshes, NPC roots + body meshes | `layer(N)` | `sf_world_bevy/src/stream.rs:1127`, `sf_character_bevy/src/world.rs:42`, `lib.rs:860` |
| **player root / avatar / body meshes** | **none (layer 0)** | `mw_player_bevy/src/lib.rs:352-371`, `avatar.rs:162-169`; only the camera traveler gets `[0, N]` (`sf_world_bevy/src/fade.rs:238`) |
| **held torch light** | **none (layer 0)** | `mw_actors_bevy/src/held.rs:255`: spawned fresh under the `HeldLightSlot`, never copies the slot's layers (layers don't inherit through `ChildOf`) |
| sun / moon, camera | `[0, N]` | `exterior.rs:652, 837-855` |

Consequences, matching the owner's notes exactly:
- Every placed lamp sees world meshes and NPCs as casters but **never the player** ("one lamp shadowed an NPC and
  not the player"; sun shadows follow everyone because the sun is on `[0, N]`).
- A held torch light (layer 0) lights the scene (clusters are per view, `[0, N]`) but its six shadow faces can only
  contain layer-0 meshes, i.e. the player's body (the torch prop itself is `NotShadowCaster`, `held.rs:175`). So an
  NPC's torch can shadow the player but never its own carrier, other NPCs or the world; the player's own torch can
  shadow only the player. Either way it holds a budget slot (priority 1, 1-3 m from the eye) for almost nothing.
  (Review correction: the first draft said "six faces of nothing"; the player's body is a layer-0 caster.)
- Not implicated: the vendored skip rule / `ShadowMoverRefresh` (a stale face shows a wrong shadow, not none), the
  cube cache (`POINT_SHADOW_CACHE = false`, compiled out, `--off shadowcache` is a no-op), `shadow_map_near_z` 0.25 m,
  `drop_stale_light_views`.

Interior NPCs casting nothing (Census office) is **not** explained by layers (NPC roots get `cx.layers` indoors
too). **Review finding (CONFIRMED in code): the budget wastes slots on lights that can't render.** `budget_shadows`
(`shadow_budget.rs:229-233`) ranks every "shown" `BudgetedShadow` light within 45 m by `Visibility` only: no
`ViewVisibility`, no worldspace filter. Inactive worldspace roots stay `Visibility::Visible` (`worldspace.rs:100`),
door prefetch keeps the other side resident within 24 m, and each worldspace has its own origin, so another cell's
lights can rank inside 45 m, take a slot, and are then never extracted (`extract_lights` drops non-view-visible
lights, vendored `light.rs:509`). Lights behind the camera outside their range hold slots the same way. Fits "random
lamps" and the office by its door. Also: the skip rule's refresh budget (`light.rs:1491`, 2 faces minus mandatory
new-slot faces) can leave a face without a newly built NPC for a while when slots churn. Older suspects: 300-nit interior ambient + many unshadowed lights washing one lamp's shadow out
(`mw_assets/src/lights.rs:63`); the 8 slots going to the lights nearest the **camera**, not the ones lighting the
NPC (`shadow_budget.rs:228-246`); torch-bearing NPCs (layer problem above). BRP check: read `RenderLayers` on one
NPC's meshes and on the shadowed lights; then `--point-shadows 12`.

## 2. "Only a few metres", "random lamps", grain

- **Reach is the light's range.** `range = 4 × record radius` (`lights.rs:66, 99`): a 256-unit lamp reaches 14.6 m
  and a shadow can't exist past it; inverse-square makes it legible only where the lamp beats the 4-nit night
  ambient. The viewer fade is 35-45 m now (`render.rs:32`; SH-13 still says 15-25 m / 1 light: stale).
- **Selection** (`shadow_budget.rs:97-99, 228-246`): importance `(range/d)²·coverage·priority` with d = viewer→light,
  hysteresis 0.8, one new slot per frame. In Balmora dozens of lamps sit inside 45 m, so the 8 holders are "nearest
  to the camera, sticky", unrelated to what the eye sees lit: the "random" look. HDRP ranks by screen size; adding a
  "lights a character / the player" term would make the pick legible.
- **Grain**: High = 8 maps at **512²** (`exterior.rs:1253-1260`, `POINT_SHADOWS_DEFAULT`), cube texel 3.9 cm at
  10 m, 7.8 cm at 20 m, filtered by the own B2 patch's **1-tap Hardware2x2** for lamps on every preset
  (`exterior.rs:697`, `--off lampfilter` A/B), lamp depth bias at Bevy's 0.08 m (detaches feet/table legs). Trade to
  measure: 4-6 lamps at 1024² vs 8 at 512² (12 vs 24 MB). Also `Quality::shadow_filter` picks `Temporal` whenever
  upscaling is temporal (`exterior.rs:418, 1321-1326`) but the native fallback that removes `TemporalJitter`
  (`sf_sky_bevy/src/upscale.rs:173`) never swaps the filter back to Gaussian: dither with no resolve.
- **Lamp housings** are ordinary casters; only the 0.25 m near plane clips them. Parts of a streetlamp further than
  25 cm from the flame black out whole faces (`SelfLitProp` rule exists for held props only, `held.rs:159-180`).
- (Withdrawn after review: `mark_light_props`, `held.rs:145-151`, marks only props with a light, so weapons and shields
  do cast.)

## 3. Camera-moving bands (RO-11) and the bright rim (RO-12)

The release start is **not** a different settings path: `play()` uses the `view` defaults (`main.rs:869-888`), so
DLSS auto, High, Temporal filter, PCSS 10 texels, sun/moon contact shadows, GTAO Medium are identical to
`scene wk-balmora`. The difference is content: Seyda Neen's haze and a close third-person character. Candidates:

1. **GTAO at output resolution under DLSS** (CONFIRMED gap): `ssao/ssao.wesl:52, 141` divide by `view.viewport.zw`
   while depth was rasterized into `main_pass_viewport`. Upstream fix **bevy#26234** "Use the main-pass resolution
   for SSAO" (merged 2026-10-26, files `ssao/{mod.rs,ssao.wesl,preprocess_depth.wesl,spatial_denoise.wesl}`,
   `pbr_fragment.wesl`, `deferred_lighting.wesl`), same series as our vendored #26235-#26237, **not vendored**
   (`grep 26234 superfargoth/vendor` empty). Its PR shows a dark band on a box stack moving with the camera.
2. **Contact shadows march depth at the wrong scale under DLSS** (CONFIRMED in code):
   `pbr_functions.wesl:308` passes `textureDimensions(depth_prepass_texture)` (full output size) to
   `raymarch::depth_ray_march_new_from_depth`; `ssr/raymarch.wesl` samples depth by normalized UV, so with
   `MainPassResolutionOverride` the march reads depth ~1/0.67 too far out: false hits on flat ground, camera
   dependent. Likely also SH-14's bands (0.5 → 0.3 m only hid it). No upstream fix found; worth a PR.
3. **Aerial-view LUT samples cut 10 → 6** in 39f6508 (`sf_sky_bevy/src/lib.rs:534`): quadrature steps at fixed view
   distances. Review: real mechanism but weak fit (gives coloured haze steps, not dark bands); rank last. Likewise
   GTAO (item 1) only darkens ambient, so it can't make black bands on sunlit ground: real bug, weak fit for RO-11.
4. **Far-cascade fast path added in 39f6508** (`shadow_sampling.wesl:362-380`; the review promoted this to a top-tier
   suspect, tied with 2): switches hard at `texel_size >= 0.15 m` (a camera-distance boundary that moves with the
   camera), 4 rotated taps with `far_plane = 0.5 × plane_slope_uv` and no margin, slope under-estimated below
   N·L 0.25, `SUN_NORMAL_BIAS` 0.8 texels vs Bevy's 1.8 (`exterior.rs:1148`). Downhill taps self-shadow flat ground at
   low sun: darkens the direct sun term (can be black), appeared in the 2026-10-28 builds (the owner's bisect hint),
   works on flat synthetic levels, sun-angle dependent. A/B `--off softshadows`.
5. RO-12 rim: the half-res cloud/fog-bank composite (`sf_atmos_bevy/src/shaders/cloud_composite.wesl:44-87`) gives a
   thin foreground object the background's bank fog (`d.y` is never depth-clipped); strong in Seyda Neen's haze,
   absent in clear Balmora. Secondary: GTAO edge under DLSS (item 1). DLSS disocclusion is the weakest candidate.

Ruled out: fog froxel/cloud passes (use the override and jitter correctly), `TemporalJitter` missing under DLSS,
fixed-pattern IGN (it rotates with `frame_count`).

**A/B order at the Seyda Neen start:** `--dlss taa` (kills 1, 2); `--off contactshadows` (2); console `ssao` (1);
`--off softshadows` (4); `--off atmosphere` / `--off fog` (3, 5); local edit `aerial_view_lut_samples: 10`.

## 5. Proposed slices (revised after the independent review, smallest first; one deliverable + its check each)

0. **No-build triage** at the Seyda Neen release start, same spot: `--dlss taa`, `--off softshadows`,
   `--off contactshadows`, console `ssao`, `--off fog` / `--off atmosphere` (RO-12). Decides which of slice 3's parts
   to build. BRP at the Census office: every `PointLight` with `shadow_maps_enabled`, its `RenderLayers`,
   `ViewVisibility`, distance (confirms the wasted slots); then `--point-shadows 12`.
1. **Character layers as one framework rule** (slices 1+2 merged: the torch fix alone copies the player's None):
   lights, meshes, props and slots under a `Character` inherit the root's layers; the player root carries
   `[0, N_active]`, rewritten on `TravelArrived` and pushed to existing descendants (`sf_character_bevy` copies layers
   at build time only, `lib.rs:860`, `held.rs:359-372`: a static `layer(N)` would hide the player after a door).
   Lights stay on `layer(N)` only (`[0, N]` lights would leak into resident worldspaces). Plus a one-shot `warn!` for a
   `BudgetedShadow` light whose layers meet no caster. Check: Census office feet under a lantern; `lamps-handoff`
   torch-bearers.
2. **Budget ranks only view-visible lights of the active worldspace** (`shadow_budget.rs:229`, use last frame's
   `ViewVisibility`). One filter, one test. Cheaper and more decisive than a "lights a character" term.
3. Split by the triage: (i) contact-shadow depth size under DLSS (`pbr_functions.wesl:308` → `main_pass_viewport.zw`,
   UV scale in `raymarch.wesl`; re-test SH-14 at 0.5 m); (ii) far-cascade path: plane factor 0.5 → 1.0, a margin,
   `SUN_NORMAL_BIAS` for far cascades, or drop the hard branch; (iii) vendor bevy#26234 (+ #26185, #26170); (iv) the
   aerial-LUT revert only if implicated.
4. **Lamp quality knobs**: `--off lampfilter` A/B, 1024² × 4-6 on High, lamp depth bias 0.02-0.04, shadow filter
   follows the actual resolve (Gaussian when jitter is removed). Measurement slice, owner picks.
5. Lamp housings `NotShadowCaster`; optional "lights a character" ranking term if slice 2 isn't enough.
6. **Spot-light sconces** (own slice, spec first). 7. RO-12: clip the bank term in `cloud_composite.wesl`.

## 6. Independent review (Opus, 2026-11-01, briefed to refute)

Holds: the layer mechanism and every layer assignment, lamps never see the player, range = 4 × radius, importance
formula, Temporal filter after the jitter fallback, contact shadows under DLSS (strong: false hits confined to the
top-left ~44 % of the screen at DLSS Quality, screen-anchored, darken direct sun), RO-12 composite. Wrong: "six faces
of nothing" (the player's body is on layer 0), `NotShadowCaster` on all held props. Missed: the budget's missing
view-visibility / worldspace filter (§1), the 39f6508 far-cascade path as a top RO-11 suspect (§3.4), the slice-2
travel trap (§5.1). Reranked: GTAO and the aerial LUT are real but weak fits for black bands.
