# WA-10 diagnosis: storm sea, rain on water, underwater (2026-11-17)

Code reading only. Owner run: `scene wa-seyda-noon --weather thunderstorm --mute`, profiling exe
19:31 (after every source touched here), log `%TEMP%\bw-owner-2026-11-17-2000\last.log`.

## 1. Storm sea small

- **Start snaps on the pre-weather default wind.** `exterior.rs:1602` `sea_wind` runs every Update and copies
  `WindField.state.wind.velocity` before the weather is valid: `sf_wind::Wind::default` (2.0, 0.5) = 2.06 m/s,
  curve gives 2.6 m/s (Hs ~0.3 m). `SeaWind::update`'s first wind snaps (`sea_wind.rs` "First wind ... snap"),
  so the sea snaps to the calm. The storm then comes in through the 30 s low-pass in 6 s steps, ~90 % after
  ~70-90 s. Log: water up at 13:00:36.3, weather valid (rain audio starts) at 13:00:40.8.
  **Fix:** in `sea_wind`, write `WaterWind.weather = None` until `CurrentWeather.valid`, and order it
  `.after(WeatherSystems::Apply)`. The first real wind then snaps (`follow_wind`'s snap branch).
- **The weather wind is not 8 m/s, it wanders 6-10 m/s.** `mw_weather/src/visual.rs:265`
  `speed: [mean*0.75, mean*1.25]`, value noise on a 3 game-hour lattice (`sf_weather/src/frame.rs:228`, about 6
  real minutes at timescale 30). The curve is steepest exactly there (p3 5.2→7, p4 8→16: 3.2 m/s per m/s), so
  the sea wind spans 9.6-19 m/s, Hs ~1.2-2.7 m. 2.2 m is only the midpoint. **Fix (pick one):** feed the curve
  the preset's mid speed (the climate-weighted mean of `speed[0..1]`) instead of the wandering one, or flatten
  the curve around each class point (e.g. p4 split into [7, 15], [9, 17]).
- Ruled out: `--off seastate` is not on by default (`perf.rs:287`, no default-off set). The fetch reaches the
  GPU: `apply_fetch` (`water.rs:504`) writes `OceanSpectrum.params.fetch`, `upload_spectrum` keys `h0` on the full
  params. No amplitude scale (amplitude 1, choppiness 0.8). The JONSWAP maths match the earlier diagnosis.
- Not settled: shore openness at the camera (`ShoreSettings` open_core 200 m, open_falloff 800 m, swell_ramp
  40 m) may still cut the swell near Seyda Neen. It was the same for the `--water-wind 30` look, so it is
  probably not the regression. **Log line to add:** on each `follow_wind` commit, `info!` the weather speed,
  sea speed and fetch.

## 2. No rain rings

- Wiring is fine. `water_rain` is added (`exterior.rs:663`). `surface.wet` is the wetting *rate* (not the
  accumulated wetness), so `rain_of` gives ~1 at once. `drive` fills `rain`/`rain2` for every surface
  (`lib.rs:987`), and the shader term runs on front faces (`water_material.wesl:233`).
- **Cause 1, the footprint fade is the puddles' (2-6 cm).** `footprint = d · pixel / |V.y|`, with pixel ≈
  0.0012 at 960p internal (DLSS Q) and 60° FOV. From an eye height h, the rings are gone past d ≈ √(50·h): about
  20 m at h = 8 m, and fully on only inside ~11 m. Grazing sea is all past it.
- **Cause 2, the rings only tilt the normal.** Under a uniform overcast storm sky with storm chop, a normal
  tilt barely changes the reflected radiance, so even nearby rings have almost no contrast. Industry (Crest,
  UE Water, RDR2) pairs ripple normals with splash particles, faded over metres.
- **Fix:** a water-specific `fade_far_m` of ~0.3-0.5 m (check TAA/DLSS shimmer).
  Then a slice for droplet splashes or crowns (R2). Owner
  check first, live: `water.rain_ripple.strength` 2, `fade_far_m` 0.4. If the rings still don't show, it is
  the contrast, not the wiring.

## 3. Rain underwater

- Drops: `sf_precip_bevy` (`precip.wesl:81` `vertex`, a camera-centred box; the only kill is `occluded(p)`,
  the top-down caster map). The water is not a caster, so drops fall through the sea. The far curtain
  (`sf_atmos_bevy/src/render.rs:1136`) already skips `SkyOccluded` views.
- **Fix:** add `floor: Option<f32>` (render-space Y) to `Precip`, packed in a new `clip: Vec4` of
  `PrecipParams`, and kill in the vertex shader on `p.y < precip.clip.x` (like `occluded`). The host (exterior,
  `.after(WeatherSystems::Apply).before(PrecipSystems)`) sets the floor to the water surface under the camera
  (`eye.y + WaterMedium.medium.depth`, or the body's rest level) when there is water under the camera, and sets
  `Precip.enabled = false` while the state is `Under`. The framework stays water-free (a plain plane input).
  This also stops drops showing below the surface from above (the water does not write depth).

## 4. Underwater visibility of tens of cm

- **Cause: the wrong body.** Seyda Neen is in the Bitter Coast Region (WX-1 "Bitter Coast rainstorm: Seyda
  Neen docks"), and `optics_for_region` (`mw_water/src/lib.rs:44`) maps every Bitter Coast cell to **SWAMP**.
  That is `optics_with(3.0, [1.15, 1.0, 2.6], 0.035)`: σt R 1.5, G 1.3, B 3.4 /m. Transmittance is 0.47/0.52/0.18
  at 0.5 m and 0.22/0.27/0.03 at 1 m, with an albedo of 0.035, so the in-scatter is almost black. Read: a dark
  wall half a metre away.
- Ruled out: depth to distance (reverse-Z NDC with `world_from_clip`, metres), the viewport (`render.rs:1039`
  sets the DLSS sub-viewport), the uniform layout, the units (per metre, no scale), and Kd (`kd_scale` 1).
- **Fix:** keep SWAMP for the swamp channels only. In the regions-map bake, give Bitter Coast texels of the
  open component (shore openness ≥ ~0.5) the COASTAL record. Or, more simply, map Bitter Coast to COASTAL and
  give the swamp its own optics where the shore field says the water is enclosed.

## 5. Gap before the effect

- **Split:** `CameraMedium::update` (`sf_water/src/medium.rs:151`) needs all 5 near-plane points under (with
  1 cm hysteresis), and `post_on` (`sf_water_bevy/src/medium.rs:196`) draws only on Under. Split has no
  treatment (U4).
- **The CPU surface is not the drawn one:** the twin is cascade 0 plus the shore trains
  (`query.rs:328`, `WaveTwin::new(.., 1, ..)`). Cascades 1-3 (storm chop, tens of cm) are drawn but not
  queried, so the lens can be visibly under a chop crest while the CPU still says above or Split.
- A third-person camera sits behind and above the head, so "head under" comes before "camera under" in any
  case.
