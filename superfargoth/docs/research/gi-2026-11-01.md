# Raster GI, sky occlusion and AO for Scrib (research, 2026-11-01)

Status: research only, no code written or run. Question from the owner: how do we get diffuse indirect light, sky
occlusion and a sensible specular answer, indoors and outdoors, on the raster path, inside the High-preset budget
(1440p + DLSS Quality, <= 11 ms median on an RTX 4060 laptop)? Numbers marked **(est.)** are my estimates, not
measurements; "unverified" means I found no primary source.

## 1. Summary and recommendation

**One plan: baked ambient-cube probe volumes per cell (Bevy's `IrradianceVolume`), with two different payloads, plus
a cheap AO fix first.** Interiors bake real bounce light (lamps, windows). Exteriors bake only *sky visibility*, and
the shader multiplies it with the live sky light, so time of day keeps working. Screen-space GI and dynamic probes
are later options, not the base. Solari stays the Ultra tier.

Why this and not the alternatives:

- Bevy 0.20 already has the runtime (`IrradianceVolume`, ambient cubes, per pixel, up to 8 per view) and the
  vendored `bevy_pbr` lets us patch what is missing. Nothing in the crate ecosystem is a usable 3D GI solution for
  Bevy 0.20 (section 4). The only gap is a baker, which fits `sf_bake` (content-hashed products, per-cell
  invalidation, mods rebake only what they touch).
- It is what shipped engines do for the same case (static world, moving sun, dynamic characters): Unity APV with
  *sky occlusion* (baked sky visibility per probe times live sky colour), UE volumetric lightmaps with a
  precomputed sky bent normal, Valve ambient cubes. Dynamic-probe or software-RT GI (DDGI, Lumen, radiance
  cascades) costs several ms on hardware stronger than ours and needs RT hardware or a large engineering effort
  (section 5).
- Morrowind interiors are small, static, lamp-lit rooms: the best case for baked probes. Exteriors are mostly open
  terrain: GI there is mostly "how much sky does this point see", also bakeable.
- AO "not mattering" is not a bug in Bevy's GTAO: it multiplies only indirect light (section 3.5). In a sunlit or
  lamp-lit scene indirect light is small, so it is invisible. The fix is (a) real indirect light for AO to act on
  (the probes) and (b) an optional, clearly named micro-shadow term on unshadowed direct light, which is what
  XeGTAO's own sample and Unity HDRP ship.

Phased:

| Phase | Deliverable | Risk | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (days, no bake) | AO that visibly matters: `direct_ao_strength` micro-shadowing in the vendored `pbr_functions.wesl`; interior env light = cell-tinted `hemispherical_gradient` instead of the sky cubemap; re-check GTAO radius/thickness | low | owner look at 3 scenes |
| 1 | Interior baked volumes: `sf_bake` step, vendored `RenderLayers` check on probes, streaming per interior; 3-interior spike first (matches sky-lighting.md open question 7 / R-e) | bake time, leaks | spike: bake ms/cell + owner look |
| 2 | Exterior sky-visibility volumes + vendored "multiply env map" probe mode + specular occlusion from the same cube; 8-probe limit handled by coarse merged volumes | shader patch, ring streaming | owner look at overhang/alley/Vivec |
| 3 (optional) | GTAO bent normals (+~25% of AO cost), visibility-bitmask SSGI for dynamic/near-field bounce | no Bevy crate, shimmer under DLSS | only if Phase 1-2 leave gaps |
| Ultra | Solari as is; disable volumes when `RenderPath::RayTraced` | none new | existing |

Biggest open risk: bake time for *all* interiors (rule: a few minutes total). A CPU path is probably too slow for
a few thousand interiors; a GPU ray-query bake is plausibly fast enough but needs RT hardware and a fallback
(sections 6.1, 7).

## 2. Our constraints (from CONTRIBUTING.md and the specs)

- High preset 1440p + DLSS Quality (internal ~960p), <= 11 ms median in exteriors, raster must meet it; RT is Ultra.
  GI + AO budget assumed for this research: **<= ~1 ms total at High (est., the owner may change it)**.
- Bake once, under a few minutes for the whole game; nothing bakes during play. Everything is a mod: content
  streams per cell, mods add or edit cells, so GI data must be a per-cell product keyed to the cell's content hash
  with a graceful fallback (flat ambient + SSAO) when no product exists.
- Time of day moves the sun (`sky-lighting.md`): outdoor baked data cannot contain sun colour.
- Interiors are separate small worldspaces with many point lights; exteriors are a streamed grid of 8192-unit cells
  (about 117 m at roughly 70 units per metre).
- Light probes ignore `RenderLayers` in Bevy (portals.md: "RenderLayers on light probes" is on the upstream
  wishlist), so probes of an interior would light the exterior at the same coordinates.
- Framework/game split: the baker and probe format live in `superfargoth` (`sf_*`), cell/lamp semantics in `mw_*`.
  Compile time: baker logic Bevy-free.
- Current state: Bevy atmosphere + `AtmosphereEnvironmentMapLight` outdoors; `GlobalAmbientLight` from the cell
  ambient colour indoors (`mw_assets/src/lights.rs`, `ambient_nits` 300); GTAO; no bounce, no sky occlusion.
  Sky env generation already costs ~0.54 + 0.29 ms at 1440p (sky-lighting-history.md, Nsight), so the sky light is
  not free either.

## 3. Bevy 0.20 inventory (source: `~/.cargo/registry/src/*/bevy_*-0.20.0`)

### 3.1 IrradianceVolume (the one to build on)

- `bevy_light-0.20.0/src/probe.rs:338`: `IrradianceVolume { voxels: Handle<Image>, intensity: f32,
  affects_lightmapped_meshes }`, `#[require(LightProbe)]`. `LightProbe` is a 1x1x1 cube scaled/rotated by
  `Transform`, with a per-axis `falloff` (probe.rs:90) for soft edges between neighbours.
- `bevy_pbr-0.20.0/src/light_probe/irradiance_volume.rs`: Valve ambient cube (Mitchell 2006). Texture layout is a
  3D image of size (Rx, 2Ry, 3Rz): six faces, one RGB per face per voxel. The docs say plainly "Bevy doesn't
  currently come with a way to do this" (Blender Eevee + `bevy-baked-gi` is the only suggested path).
- Shader `irradiance_volume.wesl`: per fragment, iterate probes containing the fragment, three trilinear 3D fetches
  weighted by N*N, `* intensity * weight`, averaged by total weight. **No visibility/leak handling** (unlike DDGI
  or APV), clamped to the volume edge. `intensity` is one scalar: it cannot carry a colour.
- `light_probe/mod.rs:52`: `MAX_VIEW_LIGHT_PROBES = 8` irradiance volumes **and** 8 reflection probes per view,
  ordered closest-to-camera first (mod.rs:270). Needs `TEXTURE_BINDING_ARRAY` + non-uniform indexing (an RTX 4060
  has them); without them only the closest volume is used.
- `render/pbr_functions.wesl` (~lines 690-760), priority order: lightmap > irradiance volume > environment map.
  When a volume covers the pixel, `found_diffuse_indirect` is true and the environment map's **diffuse** is skipped
  (specular is kept). `GlobalAmbientLight` is still added on top (only disabled for lightmapped meshes), so with
  volumes the ambient floor must be turned down. The volume term is multiplied by `diffuse_occlusion` (GTAO), so
  AO automatically acts on baked bounce.
- No `RenderLayers` anywhere in `light_probe/`. (A web search found no upstream PR for it; we have a vendored
  `bevy_pbr`, so we add it ourselves.)
- Cost: not measured. Three 3D fetches per pixel per overlapping volume plus the light-probe cluster iteration;
  **(est.)** < 0.2 ms at 960p internal. Measure in the spike.

### 3.2 Reflection probes and environment maps

- `EnvironmentMapLight` (probe.rs:107): cubemap diffuse + specular, usable on a camera or on a `LightProbe`
  (parallax correction component exists, probe.rs:452). Constructors `solid_color` and `hemispherical_gradient`
  (probe.rs:143, 150) make a cell-tinted interior env map without any bake.
- `GeneratedEnvironmentMapLight` (probe.rs:269, `light_probe/generate.rs`): filters a source cubemap on the GPU at
  runtime (diffuse + specular prefilter), so a captured cubemap needs no offline prefilter.
- `AtmosphereEnvironmentMapLight` (probe.rs:306): attaches to the camera **or to a `LightProbe`** to light only a
  region. This is what we use outdoors.
- Reflection probes share the 8-per-view limit and the same RenderLayers gap.

### 3.3 Lightmaps

- `bevy_pbr-0.20.0/src/lightmap/mod.rs`: per-mesh `Lightmap` component with UV2; "Bevy doesn't currently have any
  way to actually bake lightmaps". Needs unique unwrapped UVs per mesh and breaks instancing across different
  lightmaps. Morrowind NIFs have no lightmap UVs and are heavily instanced; lightmaps also do not light dynamic
  characters. **Rejected.**

### 3.4 Solari (RT)

- `bevy_solari-0.20.0`: `SolariPlugins` needs `EXPERIMENTAL_RAY_QUERY` + binding arrays. `realtime/`: ReSTIR DI/GI
  (optional and off by default in 0.20) and a **world cache** (`world_cache_*.wesl`; knobs: cell size, updates per
  frame, max GI ray distance; rays that miss are treated as environment map light and "leak sky lighting").
  `pathtracer/` is a non-realtime reference path tracer.
- Bevy 0.20 release notes: Solari supports `DirectionalLight`, emissive meshes, `Atmosphere`/`EnvironmentMapLight`;
  `PointLight`, `SpotLight`, `RectLight` are "not yet supported". Our interiors are all point lights, so Solari
  cannot light them today. Denoising needs DLSS Ray Reconstruction. No performance numbers are published.
- So the raster GI below is not just a fallback; for interiors it is the only path for now.

### 3.5 SSAO / GTAO and contact shadows

- `bevy_pbr-0.20.0/src/ssao/mod.rs`: XeGTAO-derived. Parameters: `quality_level` (Low 4 spp ... Ultra 54 spp),
  `radius` (0.7285), `constant_object_thickness`. **No intensity/power, no bent normals, no direct-light term.**
  Doc comment: "SSAO does not apply to direct lighting".
- `render/pbr_fragment.wesl:537-547`: `diffuse_occlusion = min(material AO, ssao_multibounce(ssao, base_color))` and
  `specular_occlusion = saturate(pow(NdotV + ssao, exp2(-16*roughness - 1)) - 1 + ssao)` (the GTSO-style estimate
  from the GTAO paper). These feed only *indirect* terms: volume, env map diffuse/specular, ambient. This confirms
  the owner's observation: in sun/lamp-lit scenes the AO has little to multiply.
- `ContactShadows` (`contact_shadows.rs`): screen-space shadow march for direct lights; already in use.
- `ScreenSpaceReflections` exists (`ssr/`); relevant for specular on shiny surfaces and water only.
- XeGTAO reference cost: 0.56 ms at 1080p on an RTX 2060, 1.4 ms at 4K on an RTX 3070 (High preset); bent normals
  add about 25% (GameTechDev/XeGTAO README). At our 960p internal resolution **(est.)** ~0.4-0.6 ms on the 4060
  laptop.

### 3.6 What Bevy does not have

No SSGI, no bent normals, no DDGI/probe update, no radiance cascades, no voxel GI, no sky-visibility concept, no
baker for any probe type. `big_space_solari` (crates.io) only resets Solari history on a floating-origin recentre.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Lumen (software/hardware RT) | UE5 | full GI + reflections | yes | Epic budget 8 ms (30 fps) / 4 ms (60 fps) at 1080p console for GI+reflections+fog; forum reports ~2.1-2.6 ms GI only | none | Too heavy; not available |

| Screen-space GI / bitmask SSGI | HDRP SSGI, OpenMW community mod (Nexus 58839), `bevy_ridiculous_ssgi` | near-field bounce, on-screen only | yes | ~1-2 ms at 1080p class (typical, unverified for the 4060) | none | Optional Phase 3; flicker, no off-screen light |
| GTAO / HBAO+ | everyone; Fallout 4 offers HBAO+ (detail unverified); Elden Ring has SSAO and, since 2023, an RT AO/shadow option | AO only | yes | 0.5-1.4 ms | none | Have it; fix how it is applied |

Notes and honesty:

- I could not find primary sources for Elden Ring's, Skyrim's or Starfield's GI internals. What is documented:
  Skyrim/FO4 cells carry a six-colour ambient cube (UESP); Elden Ring's 2023 RT patch is mostly AO and shadows
  (reports of Digital Foundry's review); Starfield is described as probe-based GI by press (**unverified**; Todd
  Howard only confirms "global illumination"). Assume Elden Ring raster is baked lighting + SSAO (**unverified**).
- OpenMW has no GI; the community ships screen-space AO/GI shader mods (zesterer/openmw-ssao, Nexus 58839).
- AO on direct light is physically wrong (direct light is shadowed by shadow maps, not AO) and is shipped anyway as
  micro-shadowing. Unity HDRP exposes it as **Direct Lighting Strength** on its SSAO ("how much the occlusion
  affects direct diffuse lighting"); XeGTAO's Bistro sample applies AO to unshadowed direct light using the Call of
  Duty: WWII / Uncharted micro-shadow approximation and calls it ad hoc. UE's `AmbientOcclusionStaticFraction`
  (0..1, default 1 via cvar) scales AO on *static* (baked) lighting; UE does not apply SSAO to direct light. Bevy
  has none of these knobs.

## 6. Candidate designs with costs

### 6.1 Interior: baked ambient-cube volume per cell (recommended)

- **What is baked**: for each probe (grid spacing 1-1.5 m, i.e. 70-100 units **(est.)**) six RGB irradiance values
  (+X..-Z) from cosine-weighted hemisphere sampling, 2-3 bounces, over the cell's static geometry (render meshes or
  colliders, albedo from the NIF base colour), lit by static lamps (mean intensity; flicker stays runtime via the
  normal lights), the cell's sun/window light if any, and a weak emissive term for the cell ambient (so rooms
  without lamps are not black, replacing most of `GlobalAmbientLight`).
- **Why ambient cube**: it is Bevy's native format; no new runtime code. Known weaknesses: low angular resolution
  (6 lobes), light leaks across thin walls (no visibility term), trilinear blending. Mitigations at bake time:
  invalidate probes inside geometry (rays hitting back faces), push them out, then dilate; one volume per room AABB
  with `falloff` (the grid is a cuboid, so L-shaped interiors need two or three volumes).
- **Memory**: Rgba16F = 48 B/probe, Rgb9e5 = 24 B/probe; a 20x8x6 m room at 1 m = 960 probes = ~46 KB. A large
  interior (50x50x15 m) = ~37k probes = ~1.8 MB. Fine.
- **Runtime**: spawn the volume with the interior (one to three entities), remove on exit. Needs the RenderLayers
  check in the vendored `light_probe` extraction (a layer mask on `LightProbe` compared with the view's mask).
  `GlobalAmbientLight` goes to a small floor; AO then visibly darkens the baked bounce.
- **Bake cost (est.)**: rays per interior ~ probes x 128-256 rays x 2-3 bounces. Room: ~1k probes -> 0.3-0.8 M
  rays; large interior: ~37k probes -> 10-30 M rays. CPU BVH (parry3d/bvh/rayon, 8 cores): roughly 10-30 M
  incoherent rays/s **(est.)** -> 0.05-3 s per interior -> **hours** for a few thousand interiors. GPU ray query
  (wgpu `EXPERIMENTAL_RAY_QUERY`, the feature Solari needs; the RTX 4060 has it): roughly 10-100x faster **(est.)**,
  then bounded by per-cell BLAS/TLAS building. Plausible to reach minutes in total, **not demonstrated**. Spike
  required (section 7). Fallback without RT hardware: lazy per-cell bake in a loading screen (breaks "nothing bakes
  during play" unless the owner allows it) or no GI on that machine.
- **Alternative bake** without a BVH: voxelise the cell (0.25-0.5 m) and DDA-march the occupancy grid. Cheaper to
  build, coarser; plan B if BLAS building dominates.
- **Mods**: product keyed on cell record + referenced meshes + lamp records. A mod that edits a cell rebakes that
  cell only; cells without a product use the flat fallback. The framework baker takes "static geometry + emitters +
  albedo" from a game adapter (no Morrowind names in `superfargoth/`).

### 6.2 Exterior: baked sky visibility, applied to live sky light (recommended)

- **What is baked**: per probe, six *visibility* values (cosine-weighted fraction of the hemisphere around each
  axis that sees sky, including occlusion by props, buildings, bridges, cliffs; optionally one bounce of sky light
  off the ground). No colours, no sun: static. Stored in an `IrradianceVolume`-compatible 3D texture (RGBA8: 24
  B/probe, or R8 with a patched shader).
- **Runtime**: stock Bevy would *replace* the sky env map diffuse by the volume. We want to *multiply*: a small
  vendored patch to `pbr_functions.wesl` (~30-60 lines **(est.)** plus a flag on the probe) so a "sky visibility"
  volume leaves `found_diffuse_indirect` false and instead multiplies `environment_light.diffuse` by the
  N-weighted visibility, and multiplies `environment_light.specular` by a specular-occlusion factor from the same
  cube sampled along the reflection vector and widened by roughness (Lagarde / Frostbite-style cone occlusion). The
  live sky colour (Bevy atmosphere env map) gives time of day for free; this is Unity APV's two-value scheme (live
  ambient probe x baked occlusion). One patch, upstreamable.
- **Probe density**: vertical extent matters. A cuboid per 117 m cell at 4-6 m spacing is 20-30 probes per
  horizontal axis; height range varies (mountain cells). Use a thin slab following the terrain (volume Y extent =
  local min..max + margin) or two volumes (ground slab + tall column over towns). **Idea, untested**: open ground
  needs no probes if a terrain sky-visibility/horizon texture is computed from the heightmap in the existing
  terrain tile bake; volumes then cover props, buildings, caves and tunnels.
- **8-per-view limit**: the 3x3 near ring is 9 cells. Options: one coarser volume (6-8 m) per 2x2 or 3x3 block, or
  sort by distance and let the far ring fall back to flat sky ambient (far detail is invisible anyway). Needs a
  measurement of how Bevy picks the 8; `falloff` blends seams.
- **Bake cost (est.)**: sky visibility is an any-hit "does the ray escape" query, cheaper than bounce: ~64 rays per
  probe. A cell: ~900 probes x 64 = ~60k rays. Cheap even on CPU; the cost is the per-cell BVH of terrain + props
  (possibly shared with collision/terrain data; needs checking).
- **Misses**: no coloured bounce from sunlit ground/walls (warm bounce under a Velothi wall). Second order and not
  bakeable for a moving sun; could be added by baking sky visibility with 3-4 sun positions and blending (Unity
  "lighting scenarios" style) at 3-4x bake cost. Not planned.

### 6.3 AO that matters (Phase 0)

1. Keep GTAO acting on indirect light (correct), now with real indirect light from 6.1/6.2.
2. Add `direct_ao_strength` (default 0.3-0.5 **(est.)**, per quality preset) in the vendored `pbr_functions.wesl`:
   `direct *= mix(1.0, ssao_visibility, strength)`, for lights without shadow maps or for all lights, on top of the
   existing ContactShadows. This is HDRP's Direct Lighting Strength and the XeGTAO sample's micro-shadowing; label it
   as a deliberate artistic approximation.
3. Optional (+25% AO cost): bent normals from XeGTAO's algorithm (new SSAO output texture and shader work in the
   vendored crate), then use the bent normal to sample the ambient cube / sky env map (fixes sky light leaking into
   creases) and for specular occlusion. Phase 3.
4. Static-fraction idea (UE): scale GTAO down where baked volumes already encode large-scale occlusion, if it
   double-darkens. Tune after Phase 1 by eye.

### 6.4 Specular

- Outdoors: stock atmosphere env map specular, now multiplied by sky specular occlusion (6.2). SSR only where
  already used (water, wet surfaces).
- Indoors: **today the sky cubemap would reflect in rooms** unless the env map is replaced. Phase 0: per-cell
  `EnvironmentMapLight::hemispherical_gradient` (cell ambient colour, brighter ceiling/floor) or a solid colour.
  Later: optionally one captured cubemap per interior through `GeneratedEnvironmentMapLight` (GPU-filtered; the
  capture needs a one-time render of the interior) with a parallax-corrected box. Morrowind materials are mostly
  rough and diffuse, so the gain is small; decide after seeing 6.1.
- Metals and lamp glints come from the clustered lights, shadow maps and ContactShadows already in place.

### 6.5 Later / Ultra

- Screen-space GI (visibility bitmask on top of GTAO, +0.3-1 ms **(est.)**): near-field colour bleeding and
  carried-torch bounce. Risks: temporal flicker under DLSS, no off-screen light, no Bevy crate (we would write it).
- Dynamic probes (DDGI/RC): rejected for raster (hardware RT every frame, several ms).
- Solari: leave as the Ultra tier; disable volumes when RT is active so light is not counted twice
  (`docs/archive/next-gen-pbr-gi-rt-plan.md` already notes this). When Solari gets point lights, re-evaluate interiors.

## 7. Risks and open questions

1. **Bake time for all interiors** is unproven. Spike: bake 3 interiors (small room, tavern, large canton) on CPU
   and with a wgpu ray-query compute shader; report rays/s, BLAS build ms, total ms per cell, then extrapolate over
   the real interior count (count them from the ESM; I did not, and vanilla + expansions are likely in the low
   thousands). If GPU baking is needed, decide the policy for machines without RT hardware.
2. **Leaks**: ambient cubes with trilinear sampling will bleed light through thin Morrowind walls and floors.
   Probe repositioning and per-room volumes are cheap to try; a visibility-aware sampler (APV style) is a larger
   vendored patch if that is not enough.
3. **8 probes per view** and ring streaming: measure the selection order; define the outer-ring policy.
4. **RenderLayers on probes**: simple in the vendored `bevy_pbr` extraction, but must stay rebased on upstream and
   ideally be upstreamed (portals.md already lists it).
5. **Sky-visibility shader patch** touches `pbr_functions.wesl` in the vendored crate (we already carry patches in
   `superfargoth/vendor/patches`). Keep it small; propose upstream.
6. **Double darkening**: baked occlusion + GTAO + `direct_ao_strength` can crush brightness; presets and
   `LightCalibration` (EV100 7 interiors, 300-nit ambient) must be re-tuned together. The owner judges visually
   (no automated screenshot judging).
7. **Lamp flicker/toggle**: baked bounce uses mean lamp intensity; large flicker amplitudes may look inconsistent
   with direct light. Probably imperceptible; flag it in the visual check.
8. **Unconfirmed numbers**: bent-normal/SSGI cost on a 4060 laptop; irradiance volume sampling cost; Elden Ring /
   Skyrim / FO4 raster GI internals; Unity APV ms cost (no figure found). Do not budget from this document; measure.
9. **Owner decision**: whether Phase 0's direct-light AO term is acceptable as a stylistic choice (physically
   wrong; offered by XeGTAO's sample and HDRP).
