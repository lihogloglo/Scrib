**Minimal set shared by all [K]:** one cycle per gait (3 for us), starts for forward and sideways/back (0, 90, 180) in the
jog gait at least, stops by planted foot (L/R), one pivot (run 180), turn in place 90/180, the other side by mirroring.
Speeds and step lengths are rarely published; the working rule is "authored speed within 0.8-1.25 of gameplay speed",
cadence rising with speed. Adult human values [K, biomechanics]: walk 1.4 m/s about 1.8-1.9 steps/s (step ~0.75 m), jog
4.5 m/s about 2.8-3.0 steps/s (step ~1.5 m), sprint 7 m/s about 3.5-3.8 steps/s (step ~1.9 m). Two steps (one cycle) are
then about 1.05 s / 0.70 s / 0.55 s, strides about 1.7 / 3.1 / 3.9 m for our 1.6 / 4.5 / 7.0.

## 3. Kimodo generation list (B3a = rethink L5a)

Tool: `Kimodo-SOMA-RP-v1.2`, text + constraints, SOMA-77 BVH at 30 fps, through `python -m motiongen` (spec
`motion-generation.md` "Recipes"). Constraint vocabulary [V] (`:80-90`): `root = fixed | "path:x,z@t;..."` (2D root path,
seconds), `pelvis_height`, `start_pose = "<clip id>:end"` (full-body keyframe for chains), hand keys. Frame: +Z forward at
start. L/R pairs are made by **mirroring** at runtime (`sf_anim_graph/src/mirror.rs`, UE-style mirror table, [V] exists;
open question 2), so generate only the left and straight variants. Cycles start at **left-heel contact = phase 0** (the
bake rotates the loop there from the cook's contact curves). All starts begin and all stops end at one shared idle pose
(feet hip-width, arms relaxed), chained with `start_pose = "idle.stand:start"`. Prompt tail on all: "... an adult, relaxed
arms, no props, flat ground." 3 samples per clip, keep the best by 3.3.

### 3.3 What Kimodo cannot do, and how the bake fixes it

- **Exact speed.** A root path sets it roughly; `motiongen stats` and the cook's speed meta measure it. A **retime** of
  +-5% lands the cycle on 1.6/4.5/7.0; beyond about 8% regenerate with a corrected path.
- **Loops.** A diffusion take is not periodic. The bake picks a window between two same-foot contacts, cross-fades pose and
  root delta over 3-4 frames and closes the velocity spike. The cook's `fill_seam_gaps` (`sf_anim/src/cook.rs:1598`) debounces
  contacts at a seam, but the pose seam itself is new work (B3b). Take long clips (4-6 s), keep the middle.
- **Foot skate.** The SEED model card [W] lists "foot skating where feet slide unnaturally"; MG1 measured 0.038-0.045 m/s
  after post-process (`motion-generation.md:160`). The cook's contact detection and footskate cleanup apply. Acceptance:
  skate under the cook limit, one contact per step, root distance curve monotonic for starts/stops, pelvis height within
  3 cm of the cycle's at the seam.
- **Which foot first.** Not controllable: generate 3 samples, keep the ones whose contacts match the table, mirror the rest.
- **Style.** Same model, prompt tail and seed family for the whole set: the reason cycles are generated too.

## 4. Reuse check

- **Bevy 0.20.1** [K, `14` §3]: blend/add nodes and masks only; no state machine, sync groups, distance matching or
  inertialization. Nothing to reuse for the clip set.
- **Crates**: I know of no maintained Rust/Bevy crate that ships a locomotion clip set (motion-matching crates carry no
  data, and are Bevy-old) [K; not searched exhaustively].
- **Datasets** (licences from `animation-sources.md`, plus [W] today):
  - **Quaternius UAL1/2**: CC0, shipped. Jog and sprint fit; walk does not. The free interim.
  - **100STYLE**: CC BY 4.0, walk/run fwd/back/side and transitions in 100 styles, BVH 60 fps, 28 bones. Best open
    mocap-quality alternative for the walk and start/stop if Kimodo output disappoints; needs attribution; measure speeds
    first.
  - **CMU**: usable inside a product, never as a data pack; mixed quality. **LaFAN1** (CC BY-NC-ND): optional-only.
    **Bandai Namco** (CC BY-NC): BYO. **Mixamo**: not redistributable, not licence-OK for shipping.
  - **Bones-SEED** [W] (288 h, SOMA BVH, locomotion included, the Kimodo SEED training data): gated; "open source for
    academic use and qualifying startups"; commercial terms unconfirmed (licensing@bones.studio). Not usable until read.
  - **Kimodo outputs** [W]: weights under the NVIDIA Open Model License; the SEED v1.1 card says "ready for commercial
    use". Our RP-v1.2 training-data terms were unverified; `generated` stays `private_only` (spec decision).
- **Verdict:** generate (B3), with UAL jog/sprint as the working interim and 100STYLE as the fallback source for the walk.
