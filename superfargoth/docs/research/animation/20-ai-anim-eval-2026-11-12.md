# AI evaluation of animation clips (research, 2026-11-12)

Question (owner): can a learned model pre-filter many clips (locomotion, combat, ambient, traversal) so the owner reviews
only the best few per group? Builds on [17-autograde](17-autograde-2026-11-12.md) (hand metrics, 175 verdicts, per-take
ranking AUC 0.54 leave-one-group-out). Tags: [V] read today from the linked page, [S] search snippet only, [K] memory,
unverified. No code, no builds, no game launch.

Takeaways. (a) Only MotionCritic is trained on human *quality* judgements and has weights. (b) Its training data is SMPL
text-to-motion from 2022-23 diffusion models; our SOMA-77 Kimodo clips are cleaner, so raw scores may saturate, and
mapping SOMA-77 to 24 SMPL joints is lossy. (c) Its own limits say it ignores physics, so it complements our
skate/jerk gates rather than replacing them.

## 2. VLMs judging rendered video

Evidence is consistently negative for fine motion, positive only for coarse action alignment:
- [Generative Action Tell-Tales / TAG-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01803): MLLMs are "strongly appearance-biased, lack temporal understanding" [V abstract]; raw video input unreliable; a 40-frame grid helped; Gemini-2.5-Pro reached only r = 0.31 (action consistency) and 0.26 (temporal coherence) with humans [S]. The authors' fix is skeletal-geometry features, not a bigger VLM.
- [HuM-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25361): VLM coarse score plus 2D-pose and 3D-motion checks, 58.2 % average human correlation [V]; the VLM is Qwen3-VL-8B [S].
- [VideoPhy-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06800): even the strongest generators fail the hard physical-commonsense split; a fine-tuned 7B autoeval exists [S]. General physics, not foot skate.
- [VeMo](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/poster/63904): renders motion, scores text alignment with video-LMs; no numbers in the abstract [V]. Alignment only.
- [K] Expected failure modes for our case: foot skate under a few cm/s, 1-3 frame jitter, small hand-torso interpenetration, loop seams. These are below the resolution of VLM frame sampling (typically 1-4 fps, 224-448 px). Gross faults (falls, wrong action, twisted limbs) are visible.

**Verdict.** A VLM (local Qwen2.5/3-VL 7-8B, or a Gemini API) is not a quality judge for our clips: expect correlation
with the owner at or below the hand metrics, with confident-sounding wrong explanations. At most a "gross failure / wrong
action" hint on a contact sheet of 8-12 frames. Cloud models upload clips (against "prefer local"); local 7-8B needs
~6-8 GB at 4-bit [K], fine for batch runs, not alongside the game. **Owner decision (flagged):** the project rule forbids
agents judging *rendering* from screenshots; a VLM on rendered clips is that, even as a pre-filter. Recommendation: do not
use it on pixels. If wanted anyway, run only on a shading-free skeleton render of the source BVH, label it "advisory",
and log its agreement with verdicts before it may hide anything.

## 3. Preference learning from our verdicts

- 175 keep/maybe/reject labels; 17 shows labels follow the *prompt group* more than the take (LOGO AUC 0.54). A model fitted naively learns "which prompt". Train on **within-prompt pairs** (keep vs reject in the same group), as MotionCritic does with choices among generations of one prompt.
- Pair budget: within-group pairs now number tens to low hundreds [K from 17's counts]. MotionCritic used 46.7k training pairs [V]. Training only a small head on frozen MotionCritic features plus our geometric features is plausible with a few hundred pairs; training a DSTformer from scratch is not.
- Ranking N items from noisy comparisons needs about N log N comparisons, and adaptive pair choice beats random ([Just Sort It](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.05556) [S]); few-shot preference reward learning needs prior tasks to adapt from ([Hejna and Sadigh](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03363) [S]).
- Active learning for us: the owner reviews clips where the scorers disagree (metrics say fine, critic says bad, or the reverse), plus a few sure ones for calibration. Each batch then adds labels where they matter. 20 keeps is enough for per-type medians only.
- Honest ceiling at n ~ 200 noisy labels: reject the obvious (what 17 already does, 58 % of rejects) and mildly order the rest; do not expect a reliable top-3.

## 4. Studio practice

No public talk found on learned QA of animation clips at Ubisoft La Forge, EA SEED, Epic or Embark (a gap in my
search, not proof). What exists: Learned Motion Matching is about memory, not QA ([Ubisoft post](https://montreal.ubisoft.com/?p=22999) [S]);
mocap pipelines gate on structural checks (foot contacts, root continuity, bone limits) then a human taste pass
([cleanup workflow](https://mocaponline.com/blogs/mocap-news/mocap-data-cleanup-workflow) [S]); perceptual foot-slip
thresholds ([paper](https://core.ac.uk/works/153922345) [S]) could calibrate our skate gate. Same split as 17: automate
structural faults, humans judge taste.

## 5. Recommendation (ranked)

1. **Trial MotionCritic as an offline scorer (1-2 days, Python, local; CPU fallback exists in its demo [V]).** Feed: map
   SOMA-77 to the 24 SMPL joints, FK to axis-angle at 30 fps, root translation, windowed to the length the repo expects
   (check `docs/motion.md`; [K] ~120 frames). Validate with within-prompt-group AUC and leave-one-group-out as in 17.
   Success bar: within-group AUC >= 0.65 and signal left after the physical gates. Expected agreement 0.60-0.70
   [K, guess: domain shift, old generators]. Licence blocker: ask the authors; keep out of the repo, never shipped.
   Free sanity check first: does it score real mocap (packs, Morrowind clips) above generated output?
2. **Better contact/physics features, no model:** UnderPressure-style contacts (licence check) plus float, penetration and
   CoM-over-support; perceptual skate thresholds. Likely improves the existing gates more than any critic.
3. **Small learned head** on (1) + our geometric features, within-prompt pairs, group-held-out CV, active selection of
   the next owner batch. Only if 1 or 2 show signal.
4. **VLM: not recommended** (section 2); optional skeleton-render advisory only after an owner decision.
5. **Avoid:** FID/R-precision for single clips; a critic trained from scratch on <1k labels; any score that silently
   deletes clips (keep 17's "gated clips behind a filter"); cloud upload of clips; MotionCritic as both metric and
   fine-tuning loss (its own warning [V]).

Other categories (combat, ambient, traversal): all critics above were trained on text-to-motion daily actions, so combat
and traversal are out of distribution [K]. Collect per-category verdicts before trusting any score there.
